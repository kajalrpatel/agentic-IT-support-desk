# Payroll calendar and payment queries

NovaTech pays monthly on the last working day of the calendar month. If month-end falls on a weekend or company holiday, payment moves to the preceding working day. Employees can view their payslip in the payroll portal after payroll closes. If a deposit is missing, contact Payroll using the payslip month and employee ID. Payroll verifies individual amounts privately.
