# New employee benefits enrollment

After joining, open Benefits in the employee portal and review the plans available for your work location. Select a plan, enter eligible dependants and submit the enrollment form. Save the confirmation and contact HR if an option or confirmation is missing. The plan documents in the portal specify coverage and effective dates.
