# Reporting workplace harassment

Employees may report workplace harassment privately to HR or their manager. If the manager is involved, contact HR directly. Give dates, a description of events and any supporting information you feel comfortable sharing. HR acknowledges the report, explains the next steps and limits access to people handling the matter. NovaTech prohibits retaliation for reporting a concern in good faith.
