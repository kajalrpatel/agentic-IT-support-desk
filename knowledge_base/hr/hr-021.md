# What is the policy for availing compensatory off in our company?

Compensatory off should be availed within 60 days from the date it is accrued. The request for compensatory off must be approved by your reporting manager and should not impact project deliverables. Unused compensatory off will not be carried forward beyond the 60-day period, and employees cannot encash compensatory off.
