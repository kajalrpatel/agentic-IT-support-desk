# Employee expense reimbursement

Submit business expenses through the employee expense portal. Include the purchase date, amount, currency, business purpose, cost centre and itemised receipt. The reporting manager reviews the business purpose and Finance verifies the documentation. Employees can track approval or respond to a correction request in the portal. For location-specific meal limits, ask Finance before spending.
