# NovaTech Employee Leave Policy

**Policy owner:** Human Resources  
**Status:** Approved for the fictional NovaTech handbook  
**Applies to:** Eligible employees of NovaTech

## 1. Purpose and scope

NovaTech provides leave to support employees' health, personal responsibilities, rest, and family needs while maintaining continuity of work. Employees and managers share responsibility for planning absences, maintaining accurate leave records, and arranging reasonable handovers.

Unless an employment agreement provides otherwise, this policy applies to regular full-time employees, including employees on probation. Part-time employees receive casual, sick, and earned leave in proportion to their contracted working hours. Parental-leave duration is not reduced solely because an employee works part-time; pay reflects the employee's normal contracted hours. Contractors and agency workers follow their engagement terms rather than this employee leave policy.

If applicable law or an employment agreement provides a greater entitlement, HR must apply that entitlement. Employees should contact HR where their individual terms differ from this policy.

## 3. Common administration rules

### Leave year and counting days

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Casual, sick, and earned leave are measured in scheduled working days; weekends, weekly rest days, and company holidays are not deducted unless they are scheduled working days for that employee.

Maternity and paternity leave are measured in consecutive calendar weeks and include weekends and holidays. A holiday during either period does not automatically extend the approved end date.

Casual, sick, earned, and compensatory leave may be requested in full-day or half-day units. The leave system retains fractional accrual balances; only available full or half days may be booked.

### Proration and pay

For employees joining or leaving during the year, casual and sick leave are prorated by calendar days of employment in that year and rounded up to the next half-day. HR adjusts the balance when employment dates change. Earned leave follows the monthly accrual rule in Section 6 instead of an upfront annual allocation.

Paid leave means normal fixed salary for the approved absence. Overtime, reimbursements, and variable incentives continue to follow their respective policies. The same absence cannot be charged to two leave categories.

### Planning and notification

For planned leave, employees must notify the **Leave Plans** channel at least one week in advance, submit a request in the leave-management system, and obtain approval from their reporting manager. For a short-notice need, employees should notify the manager as soon as possible and explain why advance notice was not feasible.

For unplanned sick leave, employees should update the Leave Plans channel between 9:30 and 10:00 a.m., or before their scrum meeting, whichever is earlier. If an emergency prevents timely notification, the employee or a representative should notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible and complete the leave record when able.

Employees taking two or more days of leave should schedule an out-of-office message and agree on a handover. Remote employees must also update the HR portal and their availability status where appropriate. Public team messages should state availability and expected return dates, not private medical details.

### Approval responsibilities

The reporting manager reviews the requested dates, workload coverage, and handover arrangements. HR confirms eligibility, balances, supporting documentation, and payroll treatment. A submitted request is not an approved request; employees should check the approval status in the leave system.

Managers should discuss alternatives when planned casual or earned leave conflicts with essential coverage. Illness and parental leave must be handled through the relevant provisions below rather than treated as discretionary vacation requests. Managers must not promise exceptions or payments outside their authority.

## 4. Casual leave

### Entitlement and eligibility

Eligible employees receive **12 days of paid casual leave per calendar year**, subject to proration. Casual leave is intended for short personal commitments, urgent household matters, appointments, and unforeseen personal needs. Employees may use their available balance during probation.

### Application and approval

Submit planned requests through the leave system and obtain reporting-manager approval. Follow the one-week notification rule where the need is foreseeable. For an urgent absence, contact the manager promptly and submit the request as soon as practicable.

### Carry-forward and encashment

Unused casual leave expires on 31 December. It cannot be carried into the next year, transferred to another employee, converted into earned leave, or encashed during employment or on separation.

## 5. Sick leave

### Entitlement and eligibility

Eligible employees receive **8 days of paid sick leave per calendar year**, subject to proration, for their own illness, injury, medical treatment, or recovery. Sick leave is available during probation.

### Notification and approval

Notify the reporting manager and follow the unplanned-leave reporting process. Record the absence in the leave system when able. For more than two consecutive working days of sick leave, HR may request a medical certificate confirming the period of absence or relevant fitness restrictions. Employees should submit medical documentation privately to HR rather than to a public team channel.

The manager records the absence and HR validates any documentation. An emergency must not be treated as a failure to request advance approval. If the sick leave balance is exhausted, HR and the employee should discuss available earned leave, another applicable entitlement, or approved unpaid leave. No alternative category is deducted automatically.

### Carry-forward and encashment

Unused sick leave expires at the end of the calendar year. It is not carried forward or encashed, including on separation.

## 6. Earned leave and vacation

### Entitlement and eligibility

Eligible employees earn **15 days of paid earned leave per full year**, accrued at **1.25 days per completed month of service**. Accrual begins on joining, including during probation. Earned leave is the category used for planned vacation and longer personal breaks.

Employees may request accrued leave once it is available in the system. Leave not yet accrued requires a separate, documented advance-leave arrangement approved by HR and the reporting manager; such an arrangement is not automatic.

### Application and approval

Submit proposed dates at least one week in advance and earlier where possible for extended absences. The manager reviews coverage and handover needs, discusses any scheduling conflict, and approves the request in the leave system. Employees should obtain approval before committing to non-refundable travel arrangements.

### Carry-forward

Up to **30 unused earned leave days** may be carried into the next calendar year. This is a year-end carry-forward limit, not a cap on the total balance during the year. For example, an employee carrying 30 days may accrue additional days during the following year.

HR should notify employees and managers of balances above the carry-forward limit before year-end so leave can be scheduled. Excess days lapse at year-end unless HR approves a documented exception or a different applicable requirement governs the balance. If company-directed cancellation prevented approved leave from being taken, the manager must refer the case to HR before any balance is forfeited.

### Encashment

Routine encashment during employment is not available under this policy. On separation, the employee's unused, accrued, and vested earned leave balance is paid through the final settlement after HR verifies the balance.

For this company policy, the calculation is:

**Earned leave payment = eligible unused days × (last monthly basic salary ÷ 30).**

Allowances, reimbursements, bonuses, and variable pay are excluded from this formula. HR and Payroll must confirm the applicable contractual or local calculation before processing payment. Casual leave, sick leave, parental leave, and compensatory off are not included in this earned-leave settlement.

## 7. Maternity leave

### Entitlement and eligibility

An eligible employee giving birth may take **26 weeks of paid maternity leave per childbirth**. This policy applies no additional company service threshold or probation exclusion. Maternity leave is a separate entitlement and does not reduce casual, sick, or earned leave balances.

### Application and administration

Inform HR and the reporting manager as early as reasonably possible, preferably at least eight weeks before the expected start date. Submit the expected delivery date, requested leave dates, and supporting medical confirmation to HR confidentially. Shorter notice caused by medical circumstances or early delivery does not remove eligibility.

The employee should agree on the start date and handover with HR and the manager. The 26-week period includes any maternity leave taken before childbirth; it is not 26 weeks plus an additional pre-delivery period. HR confirms the approved dates, pay arrangements, and expected return date in writing.

HR validates the entitlement; managerial coordination concerns handover and coverage rather than discretion to reduce the entitlement. Requests for an extension, a changed return date, or additional medical leave must be referred to HR and considered separately. No additional paid extension is promised by this document.

### Carry-forward and encashment

Maternity leave cannot be carried forward as annual leave, transferred, or encashed. An early return does not convert the unused portion into a cash payment or a future leave balance. Employees should consult HR before changing their return date.

## 8. Paternity leave

### Entitlement and eligibility

An eligible father or non-birthing parent may take **2 weeks of paid paternity leave per childbirth**. This policy applies no additional company service threshold or probation exclusion. The entitlement is separate from casual and earned leave.

### Application and approval

Notify the manager and HR as early as possible, preferably four weeks before the expected absence. Submit the expected birth date and proposed leave dates through the leave system. Unexpected timing should be reported promptly and the records updated afterwards.

Paternity leave is normally taken as one continuous two-week period beginning within **90 calendar days of the child's birth**. HR confirms eligibility and supporting birth documentation; the manager coordinates the handover. Any request to split or defer the period requires written HR approval.

### Carry-forward and encashment

Paternity leave cannot be carried forward as an annual balance or encashed. The 90-day usage window continues across a calendar-year boundary; it is measured from the birth date, not from 31 December. Any unused entitlement outside that window lapses unless HR has approved an exception or another applicable requirement governs it.

## 9. Compensatory off

### Eligibility and credit

Compensatory off recognises additional work that has been authorised and verified by the reporting manager. It is not a fixed annual allowance, and working extra hours does not automatically create a leave balance. Managers and employees in leadership roles are generally ineligible unless specifically approved by the CEO.

Under this policy, one approved additional standard workday earns one day of compensatory off; an approved half-day earns half a day. The employee's normal scheduled workday determines the equivalent duration. HR records the approved credit and accrual date after the manager confirms the work performed. The same additional work must not receive both compensatory off and overtime payment unless an applicable requirement expressly provides otherwise.

### Request and approval process

1. Email the reporting manager with the dates, duration, and a detailed description of the additional work. Copy HR for record-keeping.
2. Obtain the manager's approval of the credit and proposed time off. Leave scheduling should avoid disruption to project deliverables.
3. Submit the compensatory-off request in the leave-management system after the email approval.
4. Wait for approval in the system before taking the leave. Email approval alone does not complete the process.

Compensatory off may be combined with other approved leave, but with no more than two other leave types in the same combined request.

### Expiry, carry-forward, and encashment

Compensatory off must be used within **60 calendar days of accrual**. It cannot be carried beyond that period and cannot be encashed, including on separation. A credit earned near year-end may be used in the next year only within its original 60-day window; the new year does not restart the clock.

Management may consider documented exceptions where exceptional business needs required extended working hours. Such flexibility must be explicitly approved and recorded by HR. Employees must not assume that an exception extends their balance or permits encashment.

## 10. Changes, exceptional circumstances, and enquiries

Employees must update approved leave requests if dates change, an absence ends early, or leave is cancelled. Managers and HR must keep the leave system consistent with actual attendance. Approved paid maternity and paternity leave count as service for earned-leave accrual under this policy; treatment of extended unpaid absence requires a separate written HR decision.

Adoption leave, surrogacy-related leave, pregnancy loss, extended medical absence, and other specialised circumstances are not fully defined here. HR must confirm the appropriate entitlement before advising the employee; the absence of a provision under this policy must not be interpreted as absence of a right.

Employees should contact HR for questions about eligibility, balances, documentation, exceptions, or settlement. Personal leave balances must be checked in the authorised leave system; this handbook describes policy and does not establish an individual employee's current balance.
