# How will my performance be monitored while working from home?

Your performance will be monitored through regular performance reviews to ensure productivity and alignment with company goals. Constructive feedback will be provided, and you are encouraged to seek clarification or support whenever needed. Consistent underperformance or violation of work-from-home guidelines may result in disciplinary action, including the possible revocation of WFH privileges.
