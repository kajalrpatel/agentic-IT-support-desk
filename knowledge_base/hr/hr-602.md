# What does the confidentiality policy require employees to do?

The confidentiality policy requires employees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary, sensitive, or personal information obtained during their employment. This includes business strategies, financial data, client information, and employee records. Employees are prohibited from disclosing such information to unauthorized individuals, whether inside or outside the company, unless required by law or with prior written approval from management.
