# Submitting payroll tax declarations

Submit a declaration in the payroll portal under Tax declarations. Select the payroll year, enter the requested amounts, attach supporting records, and submit for Payroll review. Check the deadline displayed for your payroll location. Use the correction request in the same portal to amend a submitted declaration. Payroll handles private records through the portal.
