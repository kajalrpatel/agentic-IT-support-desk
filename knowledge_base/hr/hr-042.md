# Can you explain the approval process for compensatory off in our company?

Certainly! To apply for compensatory off, employees should first send an email to their reporting manager with a detailed description of the task for which the compensatory off is being requested. The employee is responsible for ensuring that the manager approves the request. Additionally, HR should be kept in CC on the email for record-keeping purposes. After receiving approval, the employee must apply for the compensatory off through the company’s leave management system, and the leave must be approved in the system before it can be availed.
