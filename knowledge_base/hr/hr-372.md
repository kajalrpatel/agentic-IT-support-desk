# How should employees ensure workspace and data security while working from home?

Employees must maintain a safe, quiet, and professional workspace at home, free from distractions. All company data and confidential information should be handled securely, following the organization's IT and data protection policies. It is mandatory to use company-provided devices for official work, and personal devices should not be used to access sensitive company data. Employees must not share passwords or allow unauthorized individuals to access company systems.
