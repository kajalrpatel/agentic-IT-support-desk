# First-week buddy and equipment handoff

On the first working day the hiring manager introduces the onboarding buddy and arranges the equipment handoff with IT. Verify the asset tag and accessories against the handoff record. The buddy helps the employee locate team resources and schedules a first-week check-in. Contact the hiring manager if a buddy has not been assigned.
